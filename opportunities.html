Amazon MGM Is Reported to Be Paying About $35 Million to $40 Million for Most International Rights to an Independently Financed Vin Diesel Film, With North America Still Open
What changedDeadline reported on 2026-11-18 that Amazon MGM Studios is finalizing an all-rights deal for most territories outside North America on Burial Hill, a $60 million to $70 million original action film starring Vin Diesel, at around $35 million to $40 million, before the American Film Market.
Who decidesNot named in the coverage
WindowThe international deal is being finalized; the film plans to shoot in Q1 2027, most likely in Australia, and North American rights are still in talks.
Who it fits, evidence and what would prove it wrong
Who it fits
  • Producer: One streamer buying most of the world before the film reaches a market can cover half or more of an independent budget up front, which makes an original star-driven film far easier to finance.
  • Actor: A star attached to an original independent film can draw a studio pre-buy large enough to get it made, which makes producing an original vehicle outside the franchise system a realistic path.
  • Production company: Splitting international and North American rights between buyers lets an independent producer sell the same film twice, so the domestic sale stays open after most of the budget is covered.
What it takes
  • An independently financed film with a star attached and a director and script in place.
Evidence for
  • Deadline reports Amazon MGM is finalizing most international rights, excluding a few territories such as CIS, at around $35 million to $40 million, and that studio interest kept the project out of the American Film Market. (Amazon Is Paying $35 Million to $40 Million for Overseas Rights to an Original Vin Diesel Action Film 11/18/2026)
  • Deadline reports North American rights are in separate talks, with CAA Media Finance arranging financing and handling domestic, and compares the deal to Amazon's pre-buy of A24's Elden Ring. (Amazon Is Paying $35 Million to $40 Million for Overseas Rights to an Original Vin Diesel Action Film 11/18/2026)
Evidence against
  • The deal is described as being finalized, not closed, and the North American suitors are not clear. (Amazon Is Paying $35 Million to $40 Million for Overseas Rights to an Original Vin Diesel Action Film 11/18/2026)
What would prove it wrong
  • Deadline or another outlet reports the Amazon MGM international deal fell through or went to another buyer.
  • Amazon MGM is reported to have taken worldwide rights, leaving no separate North American sale.

Paramount Skydance (incl. Warner Bros. since 10/6/2026)Ip rights franchise
Rumor: The Wachowskis Are Reported to Have Served Notice Terminating Warner Bros.' Matrix Rights From as Soon as February 2029, One of Several Terminations Skydance Faces
What changedPuck reported on 2026-11-17, without a named source, that Lana and Lilly Wachowski formally notified Warner Bros., now part of Skydance, that they are terminating its grant of The Matrix rights, effective as soon as February 2029, while Matrix 5 is in development with Drew Goddard.
Who decidesDana Goldberg, Co-chair, Skydance Motion Picture Group; Josh Greenstein, Co-chair, Skydance Motion Picture Group; Jesse Ehrman, President, Motion Picture Group, Warner Bros. Pictures
WindowPuck gives the Matrix termination as effective as soon as February 2029, in the U.S. and possibly beyond; Matrix 5 is in development now. The day is not reported.
Who it fits, evidence and what would prove it wrong
Who it fits
  • Ip rights holder: If the notice holds, the owner of a legacy franchise has to come back to its original author for a new grant before it can keep making the films, and the Fifth Circuit ruling means that grant may cover the world, not only the United States.
  • Writer: A writer who created a property before signing it over may be able to take it back decades later, which turns an old credit into a rights position a studio must negotiate with.
  • Writer: Paramount has already bought a reclaimed screenplay outright, so a writer or estate that recovers rights through termination can sell them again as a fresh starting point for a new film.
What it takes
  • Authorship of a work whose rights were granted to Warner Bros. or Paramount long enough ago for the statutory termination window to open.
Evidence for
  • Puck reports the Wachowskis served Warner Bros. a copyright termination notice on The Matrix effective as soon as February 2029, so continuing the franchise past that date would need a fresh grant from them. (The Wachowskis Have Served Notice to Take Back The Matrix From Warner Bros. 11/17/2026) Rumor
  • Puck lists other terminations Skydance faces, on The Shawshank Redemption, Beavis and Butt-Head and ER, and notes Skydance negotiated a new Godfather deal after Mario Puzo's estate terminated. (The Wachowskis Have Served Notice to Take Back The Matrix From Warner Bros. 11/17/2026) Rumor
  • Puck reports the Fifth Circuit held in BMG v. Vetter that termination can reclaim worldwide rights, and the Supreme Court affirmed by default this summer for lack of a quorum. (The Wachowskis Have Served Notice to Take Back The Matrix From Warner Bros. 11/17/2026)
  • The Hollywood Reporter reported in July that Paramount closed a deal for U.S. rights to Wes Craven's original A Nightmare on Elm Street screenplay, which the Craven estate had reclaimed through copyright termination. (Paramount Revives 'A Nightmare on Elm Street' Via Craven Estate Rights Reversion 7/14/2026)
Evidence against
  • Puck notes the studio could argue The Matrix was work made for hire, which cannot be terminated, though Warner Bros. said in an earlier lawsuit that the sisters wrote it before their 1994 deal. (The Wachowskis Have Served Notice to Take Back The Matrix From Warner Bros. 11/17/2026)
  • New Regency sued Michael Mann in November to have the Heat screenplay declared work made for hire, showing a studio can contest a termination in court rather than regrant. (Michael Mann Is Using Copyright Termination to Reclaim Heat, and New Regency Has Sued 11/14/2026)
What would prove it wrong
  • The Wachowskis or Skydance state that no termination notice on The Matrix has been served.
  • Skydance files suit to have The Matrix declared work made for hire, or a court so rules.
  • Skydance announces Matrix 5 under its existing rights with no new grant from the Wachowskis reported.
