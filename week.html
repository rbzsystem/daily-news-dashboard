Talent Can License Its Replica, but Studios Own the Characters and the Models
CAA and Ogilvy launched ECHO on November 9, making ads from scans of CAA clients who opt in and approve each asset. The same day Disney and Adobe agreed to train custom models on Disney's characters, and on November 13 Bloomberg reported Disney+ will stream Ozzy Fox, an AI-animated kids series, in December. (11/13, 11/9)
Potential Talent Implication: A performer can now license a likeness and approve each use in advertising, while studios build their own AI models on characters that performers and artists helped define, under contract terms the studios control.

Potential Talent Implication: A star whom the buyer needs to anchor a franchise or series can trade fee against commitment length, so the number of optioned films or seasons is now as negotiable as the price.
