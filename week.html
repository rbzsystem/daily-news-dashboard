Disney Raises Content Spending to $26 Billion While Cutting $3 Billion
Disney guided fiscal 2027 cash content spend up 9% to about $26 billion, including more international programming and sports, and set $3 billion in annual savings by fiscal 2029 after four layoff rounds this year. Its 10-K puts ESPN at 54 million U.S. homes, down from 61 million a year earlier. (11/17, 11/19)
Potential Talent Implication: Disney's added dollars go to international originals, sports and streaming engagement, so producers pitching Disney may find the open budget outside the domestic scripted slate.
Skydance Now Runs Warner and Paramount Film and TV as One Buyer Each
Skydance put Paramount Pictures and Warner Bros. Pictures under film co-chairs Dana Goldberg and Josh Greenstein on November 17, and the next day said Warner Bros. Television and Paramount Television Studios become one studio with more than 120 series in early 2027, run by Channing Dungey and Matt Thunell. (11/17, 11/18)
Potential Talent Implication: Two former rival studios now answer to one film team and one television team, so a project one side passes on can no longer be shopped to the other as a separate buyer.
Writers Are Using Copyright Termination to Take Back Franchises
The Wachowskis notified Warner Bros. they are terminating its grant of The Matrix rights, effective as soon as February 2029, while Matrix 5 is in development. On November 14 Michael Mann served notices to reclaim Heat, and New Regency sued, saying he wrote it as a work for hire. (11/17, 11/14)
Potential Talent Implication: A writer who created a property before the studio deal holds a date the owner has to buy past, and in both cases the studio's main answer is that the work was made for hire.
