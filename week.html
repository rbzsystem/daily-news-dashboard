Netflix Replaces Its Content Chief After Cutting 1,000 Jobs
Netflix cut about 1,000 jobs on October 14, then replaced Bela Bajaria with Peter Rice, who starts October 26. Deadline reports eight Netflix TV executives joined Amazon MGM Studios over the past year, and Puck reports film chief Dan Lin is expected to leave. (10/16, 10/15, 10/14)
Potential Talent Implication: Netflix has a new content buyer on October 26, a TV group cut hardest in the layoffs and no named film chief, so a project in negotiation may now run through Rice.
Skydance Pairs Coordinated Studios With Almost $80 Billion of Debt
Ynon Kreiz said the merged studios will coordinate and not compete directly, Casey Bloys won the streaming job over Cindy Holland, and David Ellison has 15 direct reports. Skydance stock fell as much as 25% to $6.78 as 471 million warrants at $12 went to Paramount shareholders. (10/14, 10/9)
Potential Talent Implication: Skydance carries almost $80 billion in net debt and plans about $6 billion in cost savings, and a producer or showrunner there faces coordinated studios rather than rival bidders.
Agencies and Producers Ask Congress for a Half-of-Budget Independent Set-Aside
CAA, WME, UTA, Blumhouse, Imagine and Legendary launched the American Entertainment Alliance on October 15 to seek a law requiring major studios and streamers to spend half of original content budgets on independent U.S. productions. The Times says the companies are likely to fight the bill. (10/15)
Potential Talent Implication: Under the proposal, a producer outside the studio system would be selling into a pool of buyer money reserved by law, and the Times reports top producers could see bigger paydays.
