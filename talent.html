A Judge Threw Out Penske's Antitrust Case Over Google's AI Summaries of Its Articles

U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta dismissed Penske Media's suit against Google over AI Overviews, ruling Wednesday that antitrust law does not apply, TheWrap reports. Penske argued Google unlawfully conditions inclusion in search results on letting its articles feed AI summaries, said it lost revenue, and asked that Google be required to pay publishers to summarize articles or train AI on them. Pending regulatory approval, Goodles stays headquartered in Santa Cruz, California, continues to operate as a standalone brand, and co-founder Jen Zeszut continues to lead its 73-person team. A representative would confirm only that Gadot is an investor, and how she is involved going forward is unclear.
Potential Talent Implication: Equity in a consumer brand pays out on a strategic sale rather than on a fee, so for talent trading name and promotion for a stake, the economics are decided by stake size, exit terms and post-sale obligations settled at the start.

ABC's Eight Station Licenses Stay in Play as the FCC Fights the Network's Suit 9/4

The FCC has asked a Washington, D.C. court to dismiss ABC's August 18 First Amendment lawsuit, which accuses the agency of a retaliatory campaign over what the network broadcasts. The agency argues the suit is premature because it has not actually moved to strip any licenses, and that dismissal would protect its investigation into whether Disney engaged in unlawful discrimination. Chairman Brendan Carr ordered early renewal review of ABC's eight owned-and-operated stations in April, four years before their October 2028 renewals and the first such order in more than 50 years. Judge Loren AliKhan set an October 6 hearing.
Potential Talent Implication: A network whose station licenses are under open review has reason to avoid programming that draws regulatory attention, which can narrow what gets ordered and makes editorial protections and pay-or-play terms more valuable for talent whose work is politically exposed.
