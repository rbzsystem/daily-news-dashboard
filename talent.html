Apple says the Ted Lasso Season 4 premiere drew 296.6 million viewing minutes in the U.S. across its Aug. 4-5 launch window, per Nielsen, and was the most-streamed program in the country on Aug. 5 alone with more than 200 million minutes. Apple calls it the biggest launch in Apple TV history. Season 3, then billed as the final season, made the show the most-watched streaming original of 2023 at 16.9 billion minutes across 24 episodes, despite Apple TV having the fewest subscribers of any ranked streamer. Jack Burditt joined Season 4 as an executive producer under a new overall deal with Apple.
Potential Talent Implication: On a concluding season of a series with a devoted audience, talent and creative teams negotiating that final deal should address revival, sequel and continuation rights and whether original terms carry forward. Apple's negotiation of a new overall deal for this revival indicates prior terms were not simply reactivated.
