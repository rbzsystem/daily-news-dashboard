A Federal Production Tax Credit Is Close to Being Introduced

Trump has endorsed a federal film and television production incentive, and the bill could be introduced in the House the week of September 14. The baseline is a 20 percent credit on U.S. labor costs, stackable on state incentives and covering scripted and unscripted work. The open questions are whether it covers above-the-line pay with no per-person cap, whether state credits reduce the federal base (Canada's version nets 16 percent down to roughly 6), and how fast producers get paid. Few expect movement before the November midterms.
Potential Talent Implication: If above-the-line pay qualifies without a cap, star salaries become cheaper to carry on U.S. shoots, which could pull star-driven productions back from the U.K. and Australia and give talent more say over where they work.
